## Welcome to Thamizhi

We are thrilled to welcome you to Thamizhi — a community organization born from a deep love for Tamil language, literature, and cultural heritage.

Founded in 2019, Thamizhi brings together Sri Lankan Tamils who share a common vision: to preserve the richness of Tamil civilization and pass it on to future generations.

## Our Story

Thamizhi began as a small gathering of community members who felt a calling — to ensure that the next generation stays connected to their roots. What started as informal meetups has grown into a vibrant organization with over 4,400 followers on Facebook.

## What We Do

- **Cultural Events** — We celebrate Tamil festivals, host cultural performances, and organize community gatherings throughout the year.
- **Language Programs** — Our Tamil language classes serve learners of all ages and proficiency levels.
- **Publications** — We curate and produce Tamil literary resources, educational materials, and digital content.
- **Youth Initiatives** — We invest in the next generation through leadership programs and cultural engagement opportunities.

## Get Involved

Whether you want to attend events, volunteer, become a member, or simply connect with the Tamil community — there is a place for you here at Thamizhi.

Reach us at [thamizhi.org@gmail.com](mailto:thamizhi.org@gmail.com) or follow us on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/thamizhi.organization).
